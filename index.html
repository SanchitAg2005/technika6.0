ARKA JAIN University

AJU was established in 2017 by the Jharkhand State Legislature and is recognized by the UGC. Accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade in its first cycle, it is Jamshedpur's premier higher education hub. Mentored by JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Bengaluru, AJU represents a community of academic rigor and student empowerment.

School of Engineering & IT

The School of Engineering & IT stands as a center of technological innovation. Offering B.Tech, BCA, MCA, and Diploma programs in collaboration with global industry partners like IBM, the school provides an environment for coding, hands-on experimentation, and future-focused career development.

Technika 6.0 | Avinyā

Technika is Jamshedpur's premier technical extravaganza. Evolving under the theme "Avinyā: Achieving Harmony Through Innovation," this edition converges robotics, coding, design, and culture, challenging students to think beyond bounds and shape a smarter tomorrow.
